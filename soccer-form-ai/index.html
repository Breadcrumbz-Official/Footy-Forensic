3. Results

What this system can and cannot measure
Reasonably detectable (2D landmarks)
  • Joint angles (knee, hip, ankle)
  • Torso lean relative to vertical
  • Limb positions relative to the hips, normalized by torso length
  • Whether the body mass is stacked over the support foot
Unreliable / not measured
  • Exact ball-contact point and foot-to-ball distance (the ball is not tracked)
  • Ball speed, spin, or where the shot went
  • True 3D hip rotation from one camera — only a rough 2D proxy is shown
  • Lateral (side-to-side) plant-foot distance from the ball in a side-on view
  • Anything depending on depth toward/away from the camera

Metrics marked low confidence are shown but excluded from the score.
